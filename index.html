To operate and improve the platform, we use Google Firebase, a set of backend services provided by Google, including:

  • Firebase Authentication (for account sign-in)

Firebase's own privacy practices are described at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

We may also use additional trusted third-party providers for services such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, security, or performance monitoring. These providers process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.
